My Personal Goals

My SWOT Analysis and SMART Goals

Step 1 — My Personal SWOT

Strengths — Sterktes Weaknesses — Zwaktes
  1. I can easily do things once I've set my mind to it.
  2. I am good at doing practical work.
  3. If I know how to code it, I can turn my ideas into code pretty easily.
  1. I'm not always good at making choices.
  2. I am forgetful.
  3. I lose concentration.
Opportunities — Kansen Threats — Bedreigingen
  1. I can use my strengths to make projects.
  2. I can use planning to help me remember things.
  3. I can use school projects to improve my coding skills.
  1. If I forget projects or assignments, I can fall behind.
  2. Losing concentration can make my work take longer.
  3. Having trouble making choices can waste time.

Step 2 — My SMART Study Goals

Goal 1 — Strength

I'm going to create at least one complete coding project that I can genuinely look at and be proud of before the end of this school year.

  • Specific: Create a complete coding project.
  • Measurable: Finish at least one project.
  • Achievable: Use the coding skills I already have.
  • Relevant: Improve my coding and build my portfolio.
  • Time-based: Finish it before the end of this school year.

Goal 2 — Weakness

I'm going to write things down as reminders and plan my work every week so I know what I need to do.

  • Specific: Write down assignments and important tasks.
  • Measurable: Make a plan at least once every week.
  • Achievable: Spend some time each week planning.
  • Relevant: Become less forgetful and avoid missed deadlines.
  • Time-based: Continue this every week for the school year.

Step 3 — My Mission This Year

My mission this year is to create something that I can look back on and sincerely say, "Wow, I can't believe I made that." I want to use my strengths in practical work and coding to create projects that I am proud of. I also want to become better at planning by writing things down every week.
